# "Marea Oscura" — Design Philosophy

## Movement

A design language born from the tension between the deep Pacific and the raw heat of the comal. Where coastal darkness meets the violence of chile and lime. This is not decoration — it is elemental force made visible, the ocean floor given typographic weight, the moment before a wave breaks rendered in negative space and color.

## Space and Form

Space is pressure. Darkness is not emptiness but density — the weight of water at depth, of night markets, of smoke. Elements emerge from blackness as if surfacing: they do not sit on a background, they arrive from within it. Radial gradients function as bioluminescence — deep ocean blues bleeding into absolute black, coral embers glowing from below. Every composition breathes through controlled tension between mass and void, between the monumental and the intimate.

## Color and Material

The palette is extracted from the ingredients themselves. Chile negro: the deep burnt coral of #E8430A, used sparingly and with violence. Pacific water: the electric turquesa of #2ABFBF, cold and precise. Abyssal night: #080808, not merely black but the black of ocean at 3am. Gold of dried chile: #C9A84C, reserved for moments of luxury and restraint. Arena: #D4C5A9, warm sand against cold water — the material memory of the coast. These colors are never decorative; they are loaded with place and memory, applied with the precision of a master craftsman who has spent thousands of hours calibrating exactly how much heat a composition can bear.

## Scale and Rhythm

Typography operates at extremes: monumental display type that occupies space like architecture, and whisper-small labels that float like sea foam. Nothing lives in the middle register. Bebas Neue functions as structural steel — massive, load-bearing, unapologetic. Crimson Pro italic moves through compositions like tide, sinuous and historical. The rhythm between these two voices creates a visual heartbeat: bold statement, quiet elaboration, silence, bold statement again. Each beat placed with painstaking care, as though every typographic decision required hours of contemplation.

## Composition and Visual Hierarchy

The grid is organic beneath a disciplined surface. Cards emerge from darkness with their own internal light sources — blobs and gradients suggest depth rather than flatness. Status indicators and pricing use the coral accent as a controlled burst of heat. The eye moves from the luminous brand mark downward through categories, pulled by gravity and color temperature. Horizontal rules and dividers are drawn from the ocean surface — thin, precise, separating worlds. The final composition should feel like it was assembled by someone who has spent a career understanding how darkness can be made to sing, every detail the result of countless meticulous refinements.
